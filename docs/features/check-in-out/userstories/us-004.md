# US-004: Huy nhan phong nham

## User Story

La **Le tan**, toi muon **huy nhan trong cua so ngan**, de **sua sai ma khong tao viec don**.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Huy thanh cong

* **Screen:** docs/features/check-in-out/ascii-wireframe/cancel-check-in.md

```gherkin
Given toi vua nhan phong va con trong cua so
And chua phat sinh hoat dong khac
When toi xac nhan huy nhan
Then luot danh dau huy, luu tru = trong
And khong phat su kien tra phong
And nhat ky ghi huy
```

### AC-002 — Qua cua so

* **Error code:** E-check-in-out-006

```gherkin
Given da qua cua so huy nhan
When toi thu huy
Then he thong tu choi
And huong dan dung Tra phong
```
