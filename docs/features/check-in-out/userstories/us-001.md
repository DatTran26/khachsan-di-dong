# US-001: Nhan phong khi san sang

## User Story

La **Le tan**, toi muon **nhan phong chi khi trong + sach + binh thuong**, de **khong dua khach vao phong chua san sang**.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Nhan thanh cong

* **Screen:** docs/features/check-in-out/ascii-wireframe/check-in.md

```gherkin
Given phong "101" trong, sach, binh thuong
And toi la le tan
When toi nhap ten khach "A", so nguoi 2 va xac nhan
Then he thong tao luot luu tru mo
And truc luu tru = dang o
And nhat ky nhan duoc ghi
```

### AC-002 — Chan khi dang o

* **Error code:** E-check-in-out-001

```gherkin
Given phong "102" dang o
When toi xac nhan nhan phong
Then he thong chan
And hien thi "Phong dang co khach — khong the nhan."
```

### AC-003 — Thieu ten khach

* **Error code:** E-check-in-out-004

```gherkin
Given form nhan phong dang mo
When toi de trong ten khach va xac nhan
Then he thong tu choi, giu form
And chi ro truong ten khach
```
