# US-003: Tra cuu/loc phong theo 3 truc

## User Story

La **nguoi dung da dang nhap**, toi muon **loc danh muc theo tang, loai va tung truc**, de **tim phong san sang nhanh**.

## Linked Requirements

| FR | Priority | Notes |
|----|----------|-------|
| FR-room-catalog-005 | P0 | 3 truc + san sang composite |
| FR-room-catalog-006 | P0 | Loc + trang thai rong |

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Hien thi 3 truc va san sang

* **Type:** happy-path
* **Screen:** docs/features/room-catalog/ascii-wireframe/browse-rooms.md

```gherkin
Given co phong trong, sach, binh thuong
When toi mo danh muc phong
Then moi phong hien thi du 3 truc
And phong du dieu kien duoc danh dau san sang
```

### AC-002 — Loc rong

* **Type:** edge-case
* **Screen:** docs/features/room-catalog/ascii-wireframe/browse-rooms.md

```gherkin
Given khong co phong tang 9
When toi loc tang = 9
Then he thong hien thi trang thai rong
And khong bao loi he thong
```
