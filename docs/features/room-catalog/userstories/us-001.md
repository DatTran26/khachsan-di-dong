# US-001: Them phong moi vao danh muc

## User Story

La **Quan ly**, toi muon **them phong voi so duy nhat, tang va loai**, de **danh muc co du phong van hanh voi trang thai mac dinh dung**.

## Linked Requirements

| FR | Priority | Notes |
|----|----------|-------|
| FR-room-catalog-001 | P0 | Them phong, so duy nhat |
| FR-room-catalog-002 | P0 | Mac dinh trong + sach + binh thuong |

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Them phong thanh cong

* **Type:** happy-path
* **Covers:** FR-room-catalog-001, FR-room-catalog-002
* **Screen:** docs/features/room-catalog/ascii-wireframe/manage-room.md

```gherkin
Given toi da dang nhap voi quyen quan ly
And loai phong "Standard" dang dung
When toi mo form them phong, nhap so "301", tang 3, loai "Standard" va luu
Then he thong tao phong voi luu tru trong, ve sinh sach, bao tri binh thuong
And danh muc cap nhat va co nhat ky thay doi
```

### AC-002 — So phong trung bi chan

* **Type:** error-path
* **Error code:** E-room-catalog-001
* **Screen:** docs/features/room-catalog/ascii-wireframe/manage-room.md

```gherkin
Given phong so "101" da ton tai
When toi them phong voi so "101"
Then he thong tu choi luu va giu form
And hien thi "So phong 101 da ton tai trong danh muc."
```
