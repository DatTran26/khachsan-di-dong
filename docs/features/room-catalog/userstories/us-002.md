# US-002: Sua va xoa phong theo dieu kien

## User Story

La **Quan ly**, toi muon **sua thong tin phong va chi xoa khi du dieu kien**, de **khong mat du lieu van hanh**.

| FR | Priority | Notes |

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Sua phong thanh cong

* **Type:** happy-path
* **Screen:** docs/features/room-catalog/ascii-wireframe/manage-room.md

```gherkin
Given phong "101" ton tai
When toi doi tang thanh 2 va luu
Then he thong cap nhat tang
And nhat ky ghi gia tri truoc-sau
```

### AC-002 — Chan xoa khi dang o hoac co viec mo

* **Type:** error-path
* **Error code:** E-room-catalog-002

```gherkin
Given phong "102" dang o hoac co viec dang mo
When toi chon xoa phong
Then he thong tu choi xoa
And hien thi thong bao khong the xoa
```

### AC-003 — Chan xoa khi da co lich su

* **Type:** error-path
* **Error code:** E-room-catalog-003

```gherkin
Given phong "103" da co lich su hoat dong nhung dang trong
When toi chon xoa phong
Then he thong tu choi xoa cung
And goi y dat "Ngung phuc vu"
```
