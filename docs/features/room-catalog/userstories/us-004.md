# US-004: Dat/go ngung phuc vu kem ly do

## User Story

La **Quan ly hoac Le tan**, toi muon **dat/go ngung phuc vu voi ly do bat buoc**, de **dong phong ngoai van hanh va chan nhan khach moi**.

## Linked Requirements

| FR | Priority | Notes |
|----|----------|-------|
| FR-room-catalog-007 | P1 | Dat ngung + ly do |
| FR-room-catalog-008 | P1 | Go ngung |

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Dat ngung co ly do

* **Type:** happy-path
* **Screen:** docs/features/room-catalog/ascii-wireframe/stop-service.md

```gherkin
Given phong "201" dang trong
When toi dat ngung phuc vu va nhap ly do "Son lai"
Then truc bao tri chuyen sang ngung phuc vu
And nhat ky duoc ghi
```

### AC-002 — Thieu ly do

* **Type:** error-path
* **Error code:** E-room-catalog-004

```gherkin
Given toi mo form ngung phuc vu
When toi xac nhan ma chua nhap ly do
Then he thong tu choi luu
And hien thi "Vui long nhap ly do ngung phuc vu."
```

### AC-003 — Canh bao khi dang o

* **Type:** edge-case

```gherkin
Given phong "102" dang o
When toi dat ngung phuc vu
Then he thong canh bao va yeu cau xac nhan lai
And sau khi xac nhan kem ly do, phong ngung phuc vu
```
