# US-003: Xem nhat ky theo phong

## User Story

As a **quan ly**, I want **xem dong thoi gian nhat ky cua mot phong**, so that **toi truy vet tranh chap ma khong can ho tro ky thuat**.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Hien thi dong thoi gian

```gherkin
Given quan ly da dang nhap va phong co it nhat 1 ban ghi
When quan ly mo nhat ky theo phong
Then he thong hien danh sach moi nhat truoc
And moi dong du ai/khi/hanh dong/truc hoac truong/truoc→sau
```

### AC-002 — Rong

```gherkin
Given phong chua co thay doi trong pham vi
When quan ly mo nhat ky
Then hien "Chua co thay doi nao trong pham vi da chon." (E-audit-log-004)
And khong bao loi
```

### AC-003 — Mat ket noi

```gherkin
Given quan ly mo nhat ky khi mat mang
When tai that bai
Then hien E-audit-log-003 va cho thu lai
```
