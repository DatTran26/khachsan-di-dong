# US-002: Ghi ro truc trang thai va actor he thong/auth

## User Story

As a **quan ly**, I want **ban ghi doi trang thai phong chi ro tung truc va hanh dong tu dong/auth duoc ghi dung**, so that **toi biet ai (hoac he thong) doi gi tren tung truc**.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001 — Nhieu truc trong mot ban ghi

```gherkin
Given thao tac tra phong doi 2 truc luu tru va ve sinh
When luu thanh cong
Then ban ghi liet ke du 2 thay doi truc voi gia tri truoc → sau
```

### AC-002 — Actor he thong

```gherkin
Given he thong tu tao viec sau tra phong
When ghi nhat ky
Then nguoi thuc hien hien "he thong"
```

### AC-003 — Su kien auth

```gherkin
Given su kien dang nhap/dang xuat/khoa/mo/doi MK theo auth-roles
When ghi nhat ky
Then ban ghi co du truong theo FR-audit-log-004
And khong chua gia tri mat khau
```
